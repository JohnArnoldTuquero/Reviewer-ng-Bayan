ABOUT REVIEWER NG BAYAN

Reviewer ng Bayan is a personal educational project created to help aspiring cadets prepare for academy entrance examinations and learn more about the journey toward becoming a public safety, military, or maritime officer.


The website provides review materials, topic guides, practice resources, academy information, preparation tips, and other educational content designed for applicants of academies such as the Philippine National Police Academy (PNPA), Philippine Military Academy (PMA), and Philippine Merchant Marine Academy (PMMA), as well as other public safety-related institutions.

OUR PURPOSE

Preparing for an academy entrance examination can be challenging. Reviewer ng Bayan was created to make useful study resources easier to find and organize in one place.


Our goal is to help aspiring applicants:

REVIEW important subjects and concepts

PRACTICE through questions and mock tests

LEARN about academy life, values, and admission processes

PREPARE mentally, academically, and physically for the challenges ahead

AN INDEPENDENT PROJECT

Reviewer ng Bayan is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with, operated by, or officially endorsed by PNPA, PMA, PMMA, PPSA, PNP, AFP, BFP, BJMP, or any other government agency or academy.


Official announcements, admission requirements, schedules, and application instructions should always be verified through the respective academy's official channels.

WHY THIS PROJECT EXISTS

Reviewer ng Bayan started as a project to help aspiring applicants while also developing practical skills in web development, research, content organization, and educational resource creation.

"Preparation turns ambition into action."
