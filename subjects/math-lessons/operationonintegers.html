Operations on Integers

Operations on integers involve performing mathematical calculations using positive numbers, negative numbers, and zero. The four basic operations with integers are addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.

Integers include all positive and negative whole numbers, including zero:

Z = {..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...}

When working with integers, it is important to understand the rules for positive and negative signs because the signs determine whether the answer will be positive or negative.


Positive and Negative Integers

Positive Integers: Numbers greater than zero, such as 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5.

Negative Integers: Numbers less than zero, such as -1, -2, -3, -4, and -5.

Zero: Zero is an integer that is neither positive nor negative.


Using the Number Line

A number line can help us understand operations involving integers. Moving to the right represents increasing values, while moving to the left represents decreasing values.

For example, starting at 2 and moving 3 units to the right gives 5:

2 + 3 = 5

Starting at 2 and moving 4 units to the left gives -2:

2 - 4 = -2


Addition of Integers

Adding integers means combining two or more integer values. The rules for addition depend on whether the integers have the same signs or different signs.


Adding Integers with the Same Signs

When adding integers with the same sign, add their absolute values and keep their common sign.

Positive + Positive:

4 + 6 = 10

Negative + Negative:

-4 + (-6) = -10

In the second example, add 4 and 6 to get 10, then keep the negative sign.


Adding Integers with Different Signs

When adding integers with different signs, subtract the smaller absolute value from the larger absolute value. The answer takes the sign of the number with the larger absolute value.

Example 1:

8 + (-3) = 5

Since 8 has the larger absolute value, the answer is positive.

Example 2:

-9 + 4 = -5

Since 9 has the larger absolute value and is negative, the answer is negative.


Quick Rule for Addition

Same Signs: Add the numbers and keep the sign.

Different Signs: Subtract the numbers and keep the sign of the number with the greater absolute value.


Examples

7 + 5 = 12

-7 + (-5) = -12

10 + (-6) = 4

-10 + 6 = -4


Subtraction of Integers

Subtracting integers can be made easier by changing the subtraction operation into addition. The main rule is:

Keep, Change, Change

Keep the first number.

Change the subtraction sign into an addition sign.

Change the sign of the second number.


Example 1

8 - 3

Keep 8, change subtraction to addition, and change 3 to -3:

8 + (-3) = 5


Example 2

5 - (-4)

Keep 5, change subtraction to addition, and change -4 to +4:

5 + 4 = 9


Example 3

-6 - 4

Keep -6, change subtraction to addition, and change 4 to -4:

-6 + (-4) = -10


Quick Rule for Subtraction

To subtract integers, add the opposite of the second integer.

For example:

9 - (-2) = 9 + 2 = 11

-8 - 5 = -8 + (-5) = -13


Multiplication of Integers

Multiplication of integers follows a simple sign rule. The signs of the numbers determine whether the product is positive or negative.


Rules for Multiplication

Positive × Positive = Positive

4 × 3 = 12

Negative × Negative = Positive

-4 × -3 = 12

Positive × Negative = Negative

4 × -3 = -12

Negative × Positive = Negative

-4 × 3 = -12


Sign Rule

When multiplying integers:

Same Signs = Positive

Different Signs = Negative


Examples

6 × 5 = 30

-6 × -5 = 30

6 × -5 = -30

-6 × 5 = -30


Multiplication by Zero

Any integer multiplied by zero is always zero.

7 × 0 = 0

-15 × 0 = 0

0 × 100 = 0


Division of Integers

Division of integers follows the same sign rules as multiplication. The signs of the dividend and divisor determine the sign of the quotient.


Rules for Division

Positive ÷ Positive = Positive

12 ÷ 4 = 3

Negative ÷ Negative = Positive

-12 ÷ -4 = 3

Positive ÷ Negative = Negative

12 ÷ -4 = -3

Negative ÷ Positive = Negative

-12 ÷ 4 = -3


Sign Rule

Same Signs = Positive

Different Signs = Negative


Examples

20 ÷ 5 = 4

-20 ÷ -5 = 4

20 ÷ -5 = -4

-20 ÷ 5 = -4


Division by Zero

Division by zero is undefined. You cannot divide an integer by zero.

For example:

5 ÷ 0 is undefined.

However, zero divided by any nonzero integer is zero:

0 ÷ 5 = 0


Summary of Integer Operations

Addition: Same signs → add and keep the sign. Different signs → subtract and use the sign of the number with the greater absolute value.

Subtraction: Change subtraction into addition and use the opposite sign of the second number.

Multiplication: Same signs produce a positive answer. Different signs produce a negative answer.

Division: Same signs produce a positive answer. Different signs produce a negative answer.
