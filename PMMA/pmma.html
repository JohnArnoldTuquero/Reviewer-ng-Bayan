About the Philippine Merchant Marine Academy

The Premier Institution for Maritime Education and Training

The Philippine Merchant Marine Academy (PMMA) is a government maritime institution that provides education and training for Filipinos who aspire to pursue careers in the merchant marine and maritime industry. Located in San Narciso, Zambales, the Academy combines academic education, maritime training, discipline, leadership, and practical shipboard preparation.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To provide quality maritime education and training that develops competent, disciplined, and globally competitive Filipino maritime professionals.


Focus: PMMA prepares students for professional careers in the maritime industry through academic instruction, practical training, leadership development, physical fitness, and maritime discipline.

Maritime Education & Service

Discipline: Develop professional conduct, responsibility, and respect for maritime rules and standards.


Competence: Build the knowledge and practical skills needed for safe and effective shipboard operations.


Service: Prepare Filipino maritime professionals who can contribute to the Philippine and international maritime industry.

Key Highlights

Maritime Programs: PMMA is associated with maritime education programs including Bachelor of Science in Marine Transportation (BSMT) and Bachelor of Science in Marine Engineering (BSMarE).


Career Path: Graduates may pursue professional careers as maritime officers and other maritime professionals subject to applicable education, training, certification, and licensing requirements.


Training Philosophy: Academic education is combined with maritime training, practical skills, discipline, physical development, and professional preparation.

PMMA Admission

The PMMA admission process is designed to identify qualified applicants who are prepared for maritime education and training. Applicants may undergo academic and aptitude assessment, medical and physical fitness evaluation, and other admission requirements prescribed for the applicable admission cycle.


Admission requirements and examination procedures may change depending on the academic year and current policies of the maritime authorities and the Academy.

Qualifications & Eligibility

Citizenship

Applicants must meet the citizenship requirements prescribed by PMMA for the applicable admission cycle.

Physical & Medical Fitness

Applicants must satisfy the physical and medical fitness standards applicable to maritime students and future seafarers.

Age & Civil Status

Applicants must meet the age and civil-status requirements specified in the current PMMA admission guidelines.

Academic Record

Applicants must have completed the required secondary education and satisfy the academic requirements prescribed by PMMA.

Character & Aptitude

Applicants must satisfy the Academy's character, aptitude, and other admission screening requirements.

Life Inside the Academy

1. What to Expect in Cadet Life

Life at the Philippine Merchant Marine Academy combines academic education, maritime training, physical development, discipline, and professional preparation. Students experience a structured environment designed to prepare them for careers in the merchant marine and maritime industry.


Maritime Discipline: Cadet life emphasizes responsibility, professionalism, teamwork, safety awareness, and compliance with maritime rules and standards.

Structured Daily Life: Students participate in academic classes, maritime instruction, physical fitness activities, drills, practical exercises, and other Academy activities.


Camaraderie & Teamwork: Students work with fellow cadets and classmates in activities that develop communication, teamwork, leadership, and professional conduct.


A Demanding Training Environment: Maritime education requires students to balance classroom learning, practical training, physical fitness, discipline, and preparation for shipboard responsibilities.


2. Key Pillars of Maritime Development

PMMA's maritime education and training develop students through academic instruction, practical maritime skills, physical development, discipline, and professional formation.


Academic Development: Students study the knowledge and concepts required for their chosen maritime degree program.


Maritime & Technical Development: Students receive training related to navigation, ship operations, marine engineering, safety, and other maritime competencies appropriate to their program.


Physical Development: Physical fitness and conditioning help students prepare for the demands of shipboard work and maritime training.


Character & Leadership Development: Students develop discipline, responsibility, teamwork, leadership, and professional conduct required in the maritime industry.

3. Maritime Training & Career Preparation

Academic Training: Students complete their academic requirements and develop the technical knowledge needed for their selected maritime program.


Practical & Shipboard Training: Maritime education includes practical training and, where applicable, onboard training requirements aligned with applicable maritime standards.


Professional Development: Students prepare for the certification, assessment, and professional requirements applicable to their future maritime careers.


Graduation: Students who satisfactorily complete the requirements of their program may earn their corresponding maritime degree and proceed toward the applicable professional certification and career pathway.
