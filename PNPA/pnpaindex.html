About the Philippine National Police Academy

The Premier Institution for Public Safety Leadership

The Philippine National Police Academy (PNPA) serves as the primary educational institution for training officers of the Philippine National Police (PNP), Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP), and Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP). Located at Camp General Mariano N. Castañeda in Silang, Cavite, the academy equips future leaders with academic excellence, tactical discipline, and moral integrity.

Mission & Vision

Vision: To be a world-class public safety academy producing globally competitive, morally upright, and service-oriented leaders in public safety.


Mission: To provide a quality four-year Bachelor of Science in Public Safety (BSPS) degree program that develops cadets into competent, disciplined, and principled officers dedicated to law enforcement and community service.

The PNPA Motto

Honor: Uncompromising adherence to moral and ethical principles in public service.


Integrity: Uprightness of character, transparency, and honesty in every action.


Service: Dedicated loyalty and commitment to protecting and serving the Filipino people.

Key Highlights

Degree Conferred: Bachelor of Science in Public Safety (BSPS)


Career Path: Graduates are commissioned as Lieutenants/Inspectors in the PNP, BFP, or BJMP.


Training Philosophy: Balanced focus on academics, physical fitness, tactical capability, and character building.

PNPA Cadet Admission Test

The PNPA Cadetship Program is a four-year, fully funded scholarship leading to a Bachelor of Science in Public Safety (BSPS) degree. Cadets receive free tuition, board, lodging, monthly allowances, and guaranteed commissioning as officers in the PNP, BFP, or BJMP upon graduation.

Qualifications & Eligibility

Civil Status

Single, never married, with no legal obligation to support a child.

Physical Standards

Minimum height of 5'2" (males) / 5'0" (females) with normal BMI for age and height.

Citizenship & Age

Natural-born Filipino citizen; 18 to 22 years old.

Academic Record

Senior High School graduate with a General Weighted Average (GWA) of at least 85% or higher.

Character

Good moral character, no pending civil, criminal, or administrative cases, and never dismissed from government or service academies.

Life Inside the Academy

1. What to Expect in Cadet Life

The PNPA Cadetship Program is a four-year residential scholarship program that combines academic education with non-academic training and character development. Cadets live and train within a structured environment designed to develop discipline, leadership, and competence.


Regimented Discipline: Cadet life emphasizes self-discipline, professionalism, ethical standards, and respect for the chain of command.

Structured Daily Life: Cadets participate in academics, physical fitness activities, drills and ceremonies, and other academic and non-academic activities throughout their training.


Camaraderie & Teamwork: Cadets take part in organizations and co-curricular activities that encourage leadership, character development, and relationships with their peers.


A Demanding Training Environment: The program is designed to develop cadets' discipline, character, leadership potential, and competence as future public safety officers.


2. Key Pillars of Cadet Development

PNPA develops cadets through a combination of academic education, non-academic training, leadership, physical fitness, and character development.


Academic Development: Cadets undergo a four-year residential scholarship program leading to a Bachelor of Science in Public Safety (BSPS) degree.


Physical & Tactical Development: Cadets participate in physical fitness, drills and ceremonies, and non-academic or tactical training designed to develop skills needed by future public safety officers.


Character & Leadership Development: The Academy emphasizes self-discipline, professionalism, ethical standards, leadership potential, and the observance of the Honor Code and Honor System.

3. Milestones & Traditions

Reception Rites: A formal event associated with the entry of new cadets into the Academy and the beginning of their cadetship journey.


Recognition Rites: A significant milestone in the Cadet Corps that marks the recognition of cadets as they progress through their training.


Graduation: Cadets who satisfactorily complete the requirements of the Cadetship Program may be conferred the Bachelor of Science in Public Safety degree. Graduates are appointed in permanent status as Police Lieutenants, Fire Inspectors, and Jail Inspectors.
